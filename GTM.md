# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Exact target user

Not "startups" — specifically: **Engineering Managers and CTOs at seed to Series A startups (5–50 people)** who are responsible for the engineering budget but don't have a dedicated finance person watching tool spend. They approved the Cursor subscription 8 months ago, added GitHub Copilot when someone asked, and haven't looked at the combined bill since. They feel vague guilt about it but have higher priorities.

Secondary user: **Indie hackers and solo founders** running 1–3 person operations who pay for multiple AI tools personally and have no one to tell them they're over-subscribed.

## What they Google or scroll through right before wanting this tool

- "cursor vs github copilot 2026"
- "is chatgpt plus worth it for developers"
- "claude pro vs chatgpt plus reddit"
- "how much should a startup spend on AI tools"
- Scrolling through their credit card statement and seeing 4 separate AI charges
- Reading a Hacker News thread about AI tool costs

## Where they hang out online

- **Hacker News** — Show HN posts get seen by exactly this audience
- **r/ExperiencedDevs** and **r/cscareerquestions** — senior devs discussing tooling
- **r/SideProject** and **r/indiehackers** — solo founders watching spend carefully
- **Indie Hackers community** — indiehackers.com forums and Slack
- **X/Twitter** — following @levelsio, @patio11, @swyx, and other indie hacker accounts
- **Slack groups** — Ramen Club, Online Geniuses, various startup Slack communities
- **Newsletter communities** — readers of Lenny's Newsletter, The Pragmatic Engineer

## How to get the first 100 users in 30 days with zero budget

**Week 1 — Warm network (target: 20 users)**
- Post in 3 indie hacker Slack groups with a genuine message: "Built a free tool that audits your AI tool spend and tells you where you are overpaying — would love feedback from anyone paying for Cursor, Copilot, Claude etc"
- DM 10 founders personally in my network with the link
- Post on personal Twitter with a real screenshot showing savings found

**Week 2 — Reddit (target: 30 more users)**
- Post in r/SideProject: "I built a free AI spend auditor after realizing my team was paying $300/mo more than we needed to"
- Post in r/ExperiencedDevs: "How much is your team actually spending on AI tools? Built something to benchmark this"
- These posts must lead with the value and the story, not the product

**Week 3 — Hacker News (target: 30 more users)**
- Submit as Show HN: "Show HN: Audit AI — free tool to find overspend in your AI tool stack"
- The timing matters — submit Tuesday to Thursday between 9am and 12pm EST
- Have 3 friends upvote within the first 10 minutes to get traction

**Week 4 — Content (target: 20 more users)**
- Write one genuinely useful post: "How much should a 10-person startup spend on AI tools in 2026?" with real benchmark numbers from the audits
- Post it on dev.to, Hashnode, and personal blog
- Share in relevant newsletters as a guest contribution
