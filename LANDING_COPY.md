## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial period. You get the full audit for free. We capture your email after showing you the results — never before.

**Q: How do you know what I should be paying?**
A: We pull current pricing directly from each vendor's official pricing page every week and build specific rules around team size, use case, and plan fit. Every recommendation traces back to a real number from a real pricing page.

**Q: What AI tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add more tools based on user requests.

**Q: What does Credex get out of this?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI credits to startups. If your audit shows significant overspend, we surface Credex as a way to save even more. If your stack is already optimized, we tell you that honestly and do not push you toward anything.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: We store your email and audit results in a secure database. Your email is never sold or shared with third parties. The shareable URL strips your email and company name — only tool names and savings numbers are public.
